## 1. Executive summary

Hexalyte is an **all-in-one cloud SaaS platform** built for mobile phone shops, electronics & computer stores, accessory shops, repair centres, and multi-branch retail businesses.

Unlike single-purpose POS tools or spreadsheets, Hexalyte unifies:

- Retail POS & billing  
- Inventory with IMEI / serial tracking  
- Repair job management  
- Warranty & customer credit  
- Suppliers & purchasing  
- Wholesale / B2B dealer operations  
- Hire purchase & EMI device control  
- Accounting, HR/payroll, reports  
- WhatsApp & SMS customer messaging  
- Desktop POS shell + mobile apps  

**Core promise:** One system to run the full shop — from counter sale to repair delivery, stock, credit, and month-end accounts.

**Primary value:** Replace fragmented tools (Excel + paper + separate POS + WhatsApp-only billing) with one multi-tenant, multi-branch platform designed for Sri Lankan retail workflows (LKR, thermal receipts, Colombo business dates, HelaPay / bank transfer billing).

- Wants control of cash, stock, and staff

**2. Cashier / counter staff**  
- Needs fast POS, barcode/IMEI scan, simple bills

**3. Technician / repair lead**  
- Needs clear job tickets, status, parts, and delivery handoff  

**4. Accountant / manager**  
- Needs daily closing, journals, AR/AP, reports

| **Content SEO** | “IMEI inventory software Sri Lanka”, “mobile shop POS with repairs” |

### 8.2 Funnel

```
Awareness (ads / referral / walk-in)
    → Interest (demo video / WhatsApp reply)
    → Trial signup (app.hexalyte.com)
    → Activation (first sale + first repair + first IMEI)
    → Conversion (paid plan)
    → Expansion (wholesale / HP / second branch)
    → Advocacy (referral + testimonial)
```

### 8.3 Activation checklist (first 7 days)

1. Create shop account & branch  
2. Add 20 products (or CSV import)  
3. Register IMEIs via Add Stock / Serial Tracker  
4. Complete 5 POS sales  
5. Open 1 repair ticket  
6. Print thermal receipt  
7. Invite 1 cashier + 1 technician  

### 8.4 Retention levers

- Daily closing habit  
- Low-stock & due reminders  
- WhatsApp invoices (customer stickiness → shop stickiness)  
- Month-end reports owners actually open  
- Support tickets / live chat from admin hub

## 12. Sales talk tracks

### Objection: “We already use Excel.”

Excel doesn’t stop IMEI mix-ups, doesn’t print thermal bills, doesn’t track repairs, and can’t show live branch stock. Hexalyte does — every day.

### Objection: “Too expensive.”

One lost phone or one untracked credit customer costs more than a month of software. Start on trial; add wholesale/HP only when needed.

### Objection: “Staff won’t learn.”

Cashiers use POS like a normal till. Technicians get simple ticket statuses. Owner gets the dashboard. We onboard the first week with you.

### Objection: “We only need billing.”

Billing without serial stock and repairs is how shops lose units and trust. Hexalyte keeps selling simple — and protects the business underneath.
